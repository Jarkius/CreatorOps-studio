# Affiliate Product Incubator Skill

Pipeline: 1 Scout (find) → 2 Deconstruct (extract) → 3 Multiply (8 angles) → 4 Test (30+ variations) → 5 Scale (replicate winners).

## Phase 1: Scout
Score the product 1 to 5 on six criteria (total 30), flag risks, give GO / NO-GO: Audience Alignment; Purchasing Power; Contentability; Commission ROI; Supply and Link Stability; Brand Ad Support. Do not approve on competitor sales volume alone.

## Phase 2: Deconstruct (Product Intelligence Sheet)
1. Core pain point. 2. True differentiator (USP). 3. Primary buyer persona. 4. Value and promo hook. 5. Top buyer objections and how to answer them. 6. Review mining: the phrases verified buyers use most.

## Phase 3: Multiply (8 content angles, hook in the first 3 seconds)
1 Direct hard-sell (price, promo, bundle). 2 Personal endorsement ("pay-ya"). 3 Problem, agitate, solve. 4 Head-to-head comparison. 5 Live demo or proof. 6 Day-in-the-life story. 7 Q&A and objection crusher. 8 Urgency and FOMO (limited stock, expiring voucher, season).

## Phase 4: Test (30+ variations per product)
Swap six variables: A opening hook (3 to 5 sentences per angle); B delivery format (talking head, voiceover with B-roll, hands-only POV); C pitch focus (price, pain, review); D length (15, 20, 30 s); E sequence (promo, problem, demo vs problem, demo, promo); F first frame (product close-up, usage, before/after). Judge by click-through rate, conversion rate, GMV per clip and a scalability signal, not views.

## Phase 5: Scale
For a winning clip: isolate its first frame, spoken hook, pacing and call to action; extract the keywords spoken, on screen and in the caption; make 5 clones keeping about 80% of the structure and changing about 20%.

## Output rules
- Analyse a product: phases 1 and 2 as a card. Generate scripts: phase 3 as a table (Angle, Visual hook 0 to 3 s, Audio script, On-screen text, CTA). Batch plan: phase 4 as a shooting checklist for 30 variations in one hour. Tone: punchy, conversion-focused, creator-friendly, Thai or English.
